## What You'll Actually Learn in a Self Makeup Course

A well-structured **self makeup class** isn't just "how to apply lipstick." It's a systematic curriculum that builds your skills from skin up. Here's what a comprehensive programme covers:

### Skincare Prep & Base Building

Everything starts with skin. You'll learn:

- **Your skin type** — oily, dry, combination, or sensitive — and how to prep accordingly
- **CTM routine** (Cleansing, Toning, Moisturising) as the non-negotiable foundation
- **Primer selection** — pore-filling for oily skin, hydrating for dry skin, colour-correcting for uneven tone
- **Sunscreen application** — the step most people skip and shouldn't

### Foundation Matching & Application

This is where most self-taught makeup goes wrong. In a proper course, you'll learn:

- **How to find your undertone** (warm, cool, neutral) under natural light
- **Coverage levels** — when to use tinted moisturiser, BB cream, or full-coverage foundation
- **Application tools** — brush vs. sponge vs. fingers, and when each is appropriate
- **Blending techniques** that avoid the dreaded jawline line

### Concealer & Colour Correction

- Hiding dark circles without the cakey, creased look
- Colour correcting — orange for pigmentation, green for redness, lavender for sallowness
- The **triangle technique** for under-eye brightening

### Contouring & Highlighting for Indian Face Shapes

- **Cream vs. powder contour** — which works better in Kerala's humidity
- Contouring for round, oval, heart, and square face shapes
- Nose contouring without looking theatrical
- Highlighter placement for that natural "glass skin" glow

### Eye Makeup — From Subtle to Statement

- **Everyday neutral eye** — two-shade technique for a polished, 5-minute look
- **Smokey eye** — the right way, without looking like a panda
- **Eyeliner styles** — pencil, gel, liquid, and wing techniques
- **Kajal application** for the waterline (a Kerala classic)
- **Eyebrow shaping and filling** to frame your face

### Lip Techniques

- Lip liner to prevent bleeding and create shape
- Matte vs. glossy vs. satin — choosing the right finish for the occasion
- **Ombré lip** technique for fuller-looking lips
- Long-lasting lip colour hacks for events and weddings

### Hairstyling Basics

Most self makeup courses include basic hairstyling because hair and makeup are inseparable:

- **Blow-drying techniques** for volume and smoothness
- Simple curls and waves with a curling iron or straightener
- **Quick updos** — buns, ponytails, and half-up styles for different occasions
- Saree-appropriate hairstyles for traditional events

### Occasion-Based Complete Looks

The best courses tie everything together with occasion-specific modules:

1. **Office / Daily Wear** — minimal, polished, 10-minute routine
2. **College / Casual** — fresh, dewy, youthful
3. **Party / Night Out** — bold eyes, statement lip, lasting power
4. **Traditional / Festival** — Onam, Christmas, temple visits
5. **Pre-Wedding Events** — mehendi, sangeet, engagement
6. **Bridal Self-Styling** — complete HD look with longevity for 8+ hours

## How to Choose the Right Self Makeup Course in Kochi

Not all courses are created equal. Here's what to look for:

1. **Qualified Faculty** — Are the instructors working professionals with verifiable industry experience? At LivArt, our faculty includes former national-level educators from L'Oréal and Wella with celebrity styling portfolios.

2. **Hands-On Training** — Theory-heavy courses won't help you. You need to practice on your own face with real products in every session.

3. **Small Batch Sizes** — You should receive individual attention. Avoid classes with 30+ students where the instructor can't correct your technique.

4. **Product Guidance** — The course should teach you what to buy for *your* specific skin type and budget, not push you to purchase overpriced kits.

5. **Post-Course Support** — Can you reach out to instructors after the course if you're struggling with a technique? Good academies maintain that relationship.

6. **Portfolio Practice** — Even in a self makeup course, you should leave with photos of your completed looks for personal reference and social media.

**Q: How long does a typical self makeup course take to complete?**
A: Most self makeup courses in Kochi range from 2 to 4 weeks, with sessions of 2–3 hours each. Weekend-intensive formats can compress the same curriculum into 4 days.
